Main operator

Goals, penalties, goalies, timeouts, game status, shootout, best players. Keyboard or touch – both work; switch at the top (Touch / Keyboard).

Before the game

  1. Log in to Hydra. Laptop: click Open game entry ↗ in Hydra's menu. iPad / Android: your Hydra game entry bookmark or home-screen icon.
  2. Pick the game: season → competition → game. If it says there is no line-up, enter it in Aquila first.
  3. Is someone counting shots on a second device? Then tick More (M) → Settings → Shots are counted on the shots bench.
  4. Set the clock to the scoreboard: click it (or C), type 2000, Enter. Start / stop it with Space. Every entry takes its time from this clock; you can change the time in each form.
  5. N → Start pre-game at warm-up, End pre-game, then Start period 1 at the face-off.

End of a period

N → End period. It warns if a team has more goals than shots. N again to start the next period: the clock is set and started.

End of the game

  • Tied: N offers overtime, then the shootout (U14: straight to the shootout).
  • Shootout (O): who shoots first, the goalies, and up to 5 shooters per team in order → Store and start. Each attempt: G scored, S saved, P/H/L/R missed (post / high / left / right), X lost the puck. When it is decided: Mark game-winning goal. Level after every shooter: Nominate next shooters.
  • M → attendance and best players.
  • N → Game complete. Penalties still open in Hydra are listed: End all first.

During play

G GoalTeam H/A · scorer's number · assist numbers (or U unassisted) · Enter. The situation (EQ / PP / SH) is suggested from the penalty box; change with E/P/S. A power-play goal ends the minor by itself.
P PenaltyTeam · player's number (bench penalty: T) · type the infraction (trip, hook, HI-ST…) · Enter · Enter. Coincidental and penalty shot are at the bottom.
←/→ ShotOne shot on goal for home / away. S or Shot by… to pick the shooter. Goals add a shot by themselves.
K GoalieTeam · the new goalie's number, or E to pull the goalie (empty net).

Penalty boxWhen a penalty's time is up it moves to Time's up: tap End at … (or End all). Hydra keeps penalties open until they are ended.
M MoreVideo review / coach's challenge, attendance, best players, Hydra chat, settings.
MistakesRight after saving: Undo in the message at the bottom (3 seconds). Later: tap the entry in Game actions to edit or delete it (delete = tap twice).
Numbers: just type them – 81 picks #81. Esc closes a form without saving. ? shows every shortcut.

Shots bench

Shots on goal and face-offs, on your own device, next to the main operator. Open the Hydra shots bench bookmark (or …/IIHF_Core/hydra-ui?mode=bench, or Shots bench in the main screen's top bar) and pick the game.

Shots

  • Tap the shooter's number for each shot on goal. +1 shot when you didn't see who shot (only if the line-up has the #115 "unknown shooter" player). Keyboard: ← / →.
  • A goal counts as a shot – tap the scorer too, if the main operator has Shots are counted on the shots bench switched on (otherwise the main screen adds it; agree on this before the game).
  • Wrong tap: Undo last shot (Z), or Correct counts (−1) and tap the player.
  • The period follows the game in Hydra. To fix an earlier period, pick it at the top (a yellow note shows), then go back.
A shot on goal is one that would have gone in if the goalie hadn't stopped it – and every goal. Not: wide or over, the post or crossbar, shots blocked by a skater.

Face-offs

  1. Tap each team's centre once (Centre shows on the number). They stay picked until you change them.
  2. After each face-off tap ESP won / GBR won (keyboard ← / →).
  3. New line on the ice: tap the new centre first.
  4. Wrong tap: Undo last face-off (Z).

Won–lost shows on each player's number. Everything is saved to Gemini Light a moment after each tap.

When something goes wrong

The badge at the top right tells you how things stand: Saved · Saving… · Offline · Log in to Hydra · N not saved.

No internetKeep entering as normal. Entries are kept on the device and sent by themselves, in order, when the connection is back. Don't clear the browser's data while it says waiting.
"Your Hydra login has expired"Open Hydra in another tab (link in the message), log in, come back. Waiting entries are sent by themselves.
Page reloaded / tab closedOpen the game entry again: it goes straight back to the game. Entries that were waiting are checked against Hydra and sent once – never twice.
"Hydra refused it"Click the badge: the entry and Hydra's reason are listed. Fix it and Retry, or Discard. Entries after it wait until then.
Clock out of stepClick it (C) and type the scoreboard time. Times already saved can be fixed by editing the entry.
Wrong game statusStatus steps go to Hydra straight away; ask the Hydra administrator to correct one.
Two people must not enter the same thing: one main operator per game. The shots bench only does shots and face-offs.
